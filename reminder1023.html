REMINDER · OCTOBER 23, 2026

Discover Korea’s Next Animation Stories

Two days, seven studios, one room in Toronto · November 3–4

 

A chance to explore new opportunities in Korean animation firsthand will be held on November 3–4 in Toronto, Canada. “K-Animation Global Showcase in Canada” is an event dedicated to advancing international co-production, investment, and global business opportunities for Korean animation projects. Featuring pitching presentations and business meetings, the showcase offers promising Korean projects a valuable opportunity to meet directly with North American production companies and buyers.

Seven Korean animation companies will present their projects, IPs, and production capabilities in Toronto. Four of their interviews are already live, and the remaining three follow on October 26, 28, and 29. For industry professionals in North America and worldwide seeking new global IPs and co-production partners, this is an opportunity not to be missed. Register now to request one‑on‑one meetings with the studios.
