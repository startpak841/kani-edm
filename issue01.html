K-ANIMATION, Crafted Globally

Meet 7 K-ANIMATION Studios in Toronto This November

 

Following in the footsteps of K-Pop and K-Drama, “K-Animation” is gaining global attention as a new cultural brand of Korea. With strong in-house production capabilities, Korea’s animation industry is developing new projects based on diverse source IPs, including webtoons, comics, web novels, and games, while forging stronger connections with global markets. Aligned with this growth trend, the Korean government is also providing support for production and overseas expansion while seeking to further expand these efforts.

A chance to explore these opportunities firsthand will be held on November 3–4 in Toronto, Canada. “K-Animation Global Showcase in Canada” is an event dedicated to advancing international co-production, investment, and global business opportunities for Korean animation projects. Featuring pitching presentations and business meetings, the showcase offers promising Korean projects a valuable opportunity to meet directly with North American production companies and buyers.

This year, seven Korean animation companies will be presenting their projects, IPs, and production capabilities. Starting with our next issue, we will introduce the participating studios one by one through interviews. This is an opportunity not to be missed, especially for industry professionals in North America and worldwide seeking new global IPs and co-production partners. Register now to request one-on-one meetings with the studios.

“This showcase serves both as an opportunity to introduce outstanding Korean animation projects to the North American market and as a business platform that can lead to substantive co-production discussions. We will actively support participating companies in exploring concrete opportunities for collaboration with local buyers and production companies.”

Yoon Sang Eum

Director of the Content IP Business Division, Korea Creative Content Agency (KOCCA)
